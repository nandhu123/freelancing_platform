About FreelanceHub

FreelanceHub is a modern freelancing platform built using Django. It helps recruiters hire talented freelancers while giving freelancers opportunities to work on exciting projects worldwide.

The platform provides dashboards, job applications, interview scheduling, notifications and messaging features to simplify hiring.
